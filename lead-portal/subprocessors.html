How we vet sub-processors

Before we add a sub-processor, we evaluate:

  • Their data-protection certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR DPA available)
  • Their security practices (encryption at rest and in transit, breach notification timelines)
  • Their stance on training AI models on customer data — we choose providers that contractually exclude this
  • Their sub-processor transparency

Notification of changes

We update this page whenever sub-processors change. Material additions are also communicated to active users by email at least 30 days before the new sub-processor begins processing your data, giving you the opportunity to object before the change.

Subscribe to changes by emailing privacy@cwleaders.com with subject "subprocessor updates."

Local-first AI

We minimize sub-processor data exposure by running most AI features on your device, not in the cloud. Whisper transcription, Tesseract OCR, CLIP scene detection, and selfie segmentation all execute locally and never send data to a sub-processor.
