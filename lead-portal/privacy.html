Privacy Policy.

In plain English. No dark patterns. Your data is yours, we never sell it, and we never use it to train AI models. Anything unclear: privacy@cwleaders.com.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

1. The short version

  • We collect only what we need to operate the Services for you.
  • We never sell your data — to anyone, ever.
  • We never use your data to train AI models — ours or anyone else's.
  • We give you full rights — access, correction, deletion, export — regardless of where you live.
  • We default to local-first — recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

2. Who is responsible

CW Leaders ("we," "us," "our") is the data controller for personal data we collect through our Services. Contact: privacy@cwleaders.com.

3. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

3.1 Account data

What: email address, display name (optional), authentication tokens, plan tier, persona, and timestamps for account events.

Why: to create and operate your account, authenticate sign-ins, deliver the right features for your plan.

Legal basis: contract (to provide the Services you signed up for); legitimate interest (security and abuse prevention).

3.2 Content you create

What: screen recordings, files you upload, applications you submit, mind-map nodes, agent configurations.

Why: to store and serve back to you exactly as you intend.

Legal basis: contract; consent (where you opt into cloud sync).

Local-first by default: the desktop app stores recordings on your device. Nothing uploads to our servers unless you explicitly opt in to cloud sync or share a recording.

3.3 Payment data

What: we never see or store your full payment card. Stripe handles all payment processing and provides us with a customer ID, the last 4 digits of the card, and transaction metadata.

Why: to enable purchases and subscription billing.

Legal basis: contract; legal obligation (tax records).

3.4 Usage telemetry

What: hashed IP addresses, user-agent strings, page paths, feature events (e.g., "agent armed," "file shared"), error reports.

Why: to operate the Services reliably, detect and stop abuse, debug errors, and improve product quality.

Legal basis: legitimate interest, balanced against your privacy.

We do not use third-party analytics that fingerprint or track users across the internet.

3.5 Workforce-monitoring data (Command tier only)

What: with explicit organization-level activation and per-employee consent, Command collects screen-context summaries, application focus times, and security-event clips on monitored devices.

Why: to provide visibility to authorized managers within the activating organization.

Legal basis: the activating organization's lawful basis (typically contract with the employee + legitimate interest), backed by their representations and warranties to us. Audio is never recorded. Off-clock activity is automatically excluded.

3.6 Hiring data (MyHire)

What: name, contact info, work history, application narrative, optional resume / cover letter / Skill Check recording, and a hashed IP for fraud prevention.

Why: to evaluate the application and contact you about it.

Legal basis: consent (you submit voluntarily); legitimate interest (fraud prevention).

Retention: up to 24 months unless you request deletion sooner.

3.7 Cookies and storage

We use a small number of strictly-necessary cookies and browser-storage entries — see our Cookie Policy. We do not use advertising, marketing, or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. What we do not do with your data

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data — to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use Your Content (recordings, files, applications, AI conversations) to train machine-learning models, ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not condition use of the Services on accepting marketing emails.

5. Sub-processors

To operate the Services, we share necessary data with vetted sub-processors under contractual data-protection terms. The complete list is published at /subprocessors and updated whenever it changes.

6. International transfers

Our infrastructure currently runs primarily on AWS in the United States. If you are accessing the Services from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our sub-processors operate. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent transfer mechanisms where required by your local law.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described, or as required by law:

  • Account data: as long as your account is active, plus 90 days for closure processing, or until you request earlier deletion.
  • Recordings, files, mind-maps: as long as you keep them. Anonymous file uploads expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • Payment records: 7 years (tax/audit obligation).
  • Telemetry / logs: 30 days hot, 90 days warm, then deleted.
  • Hiring applications: up to 24 months unless deleted earlier.
  • Workforce telemetry (Command): per the activating organization's retention policy, capped at 12 months by default.

8. Your rights — wherever you are

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Port your data to another service in a structured format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority

We honor these rights for everyone — not just residents of the EU, UK, California, Brazil, Canada, or anywhere else where they're legally required. To exercise them, email privacy@cwleaders.com with subject "data request." We respond within 30 days.

For California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have specific rights including the right to know, delete, correct, and limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Use the link in our footer or email privacy@cwleaders.com with subject "Do Not Sell or Share — California."

For EU/UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have additional rights including the right not to be subject to automated decision-making with legal effects. We do not make automated decisions with legal effects on you.

9. Security

We protect your data with:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit on every endpoint
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for stored files and database records
  • HS256-signed JWTs with regular rotation, machine-bound license tokens
  • Strict CSP, SameSite cookies, and SOC2-aligned access controls on production systems
  • Web Application Firewall and rate-limiting at the edge
  • Continuous logging and anomaly detection

If you suspect a security issue, please report it to security@cwleaders.com — we run a responsible-disclosure program and credit reporters publicly when desired.

10. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact privacy@cwleaders.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. For material changes we'll notify active users by email and in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page reflects the most recent version.
