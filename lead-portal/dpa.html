Data Processing Agreement.

For organizations whose use of CW Leaders Services requires a written DPA under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws. This standard DPA is auto-incorporated into our Terms when you activate an Enterprise org.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Definitions

"Customer" means the organization that has activated an Enterprise plan or otherwise contracted with CW Leaders. "Provider" means CW Leaders. "Services" means the products and platform described in our Terms. "Customer Personal Data" means personal data that Provider processes on behalf of Customer in the course of providing the Services. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in Customer's underlying contract or in applicable data-protection law (collectively, "Data Protection Law").

2. Roles

For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the Controller (or independent Controller as applicable) and Provider is the Processor. Each party will comply with applicable Data Protection Law in performance of its respective obligations.

3. Scope and instructions

Provider will Process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer. Customer's use of the Services constitutes those instructions. Provider will inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable Data Protection Law.

4. Subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose

Subject matter: the provision of the Services as described in our Terms.
Duration: for as long as Customer has an active subscription or as required by law for limited residual records (billing, legal hold).
Nature: hosting, transmission, storage, retrieval, deletion, and other processing operations described in the Privacy Policy.
Purpose: delivering the Services Customer subscribed to.
Categories of data subjects: Customer's employees, contractors, applicants, and end users.
Categories of personal data: account identifiers, authentication credentials, content uploaded or generated, application materials, telemetry, payment metadata.

5. Confidentiality

Provider ensures that personnel authorized to Process Customer Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations, which survive termination of their engagement.

6. Security measures

Provider implements appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit; AES-256 encryption at rest
  • Strict access controls based on least privilege
  • Network segmentation and Web Application Firewall
  • Security monitoring, logging, and incident response procedures
  • Regular vulnerability scanning and patch management
  • Personnel security training
  • Documented business-continuity and disaster-recovery plans

7. Sub-processors

Customer authorizes Provider to engage the sub-processors listed at /subprocessors. Provider will impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor at least equivalent to those in this DPA. Provider remains responsible for sub-processor performance.

Provider will give Customer at least 30 days' notice before engaging a new sub-processor. Customer may object on reasonable grounds; if the parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected portion of the Services.

8. Data subject rights

Provider will, taking into account the nature of the Processing, assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, to fulfill Customer's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights. Many such requests can be fulfilled by Customer directly through Service controls without Provider intervention.

9. Personal data breach notification

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay (and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware) of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data. Notification will include, to the extent known: the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to mitigate it.

10. Data Protection Impact Assessments

Provider will provide reasonable assistance to Customer with any data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities required under Data Protection Law.

11. Audits

Once per year, on at least 30 days' written notice, Customer may request an audit of Provider's compliance with this DPA. Provider may satisfy this obligation by providing third-party audit reports (such as SOC 2 reports) or by responding to a written security questionnaire. Audits are at Customer's expense, conducted under reasonable confidentiality, and may not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations.

12. International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to a country not recognized as providing adequate protection, the parties agree the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller to Processor) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum are hereby incorporated by reference and govern those transfers. Provider has made and maintains the additional safeguards required by Schrems II analysis.

13. Return or deletion

Upon termination of the Services, Provider will, at Customer's choice, delete or return all Customer Personal Data within 90 days, except as required by law. Provider's standard data-export tools satisfy this obligation.

14. Workforce monitoring (Command tier)

If Customer enables Command-tier features:

  • Customer represents that it has established a lawful basis under all applicable laws for the workforce monitoring activities
  • Customer has obtained and recorded informed consent from each monitored individual
  • Customer has provided clear written notice of the monitoring activities
  • Customer indemnifies Provider against claims arising from non-compliant deployment

15. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the underlying Terms. Nothing in this DPA limits a party's liability to data subjects under applicable Data Protection Law.

16. Order of precedence

In the event of conflict between this DPA and the underlying Terms, this DPA controls with respect to data-protection matters.

17. CCPA / CPRA addendum

For Customers subject to CCPA/CPRA, Provider acts as a "Service Provider" as defined in CCPA/CPRA. Provider will not (a) sell or share Personal Information, (b) retain, use, or disclose Personal Information outside the direct business relationship with Customer, or (c) combine Personal Information with information received from another source except as permitted by CCPA/CPRA Regulations § 7050(b).

18. Counterpart execution

Activation of an Enterprise organization, or written acceptance via email or signed counterpart, constitutes execution of this DPA by both parties.
