Acceptable Use Policy.

Plain-English rules for using CW Leaders Services. Most apply to everyone; some apply only to specific tiers (Workforce monitoring, MyHire). This AUP is incorporated into our Terms of Service.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Permitted uses

You may use CW Leaders Services to:

  • Record your screen, generate share links, and send files for legitimate professional, educational, or personal purposes;
  • Run AI agents on content you own or have the right to process;
  • Receive job applications and conduct hiring on MyHire, with applicants' informed knowledge;
  • Monitor your own workforce on the Command tier only in compliance with applicable workforce-monitoring laws and with documented per-employee consent (see Section 14 of our DPA).

2. Prohibited uses

You will not, and will not allow any user to:

2.1 Illegal activity

  • Use the Services to commit, assist, or further any illegal activity in any jurisdiction;
  • Violate intellectual-property rights, privacy rights, or contractual rights of any person;
  • Distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM); attempts will be reported to NCMEC and law enforcement immediately;
  • Distribute malware, ransomware, or any other malicious code;
  • Engage in fraud, identity theft, or financial deception.

2.2 Privacy & surveillance abuse

  • Record any person without their knowledge and consent where consent is required by law (one-party-consent and two-party-consent jurisdictions both apply — when in doubt, get consent);
  • Activate Command tier workforce monitoring without written notice and per-employee consent;
  • Use AI agents to generate non-consensual intimate imagery, deepfakes for impersonation, or content designed to harass a specific individual;
  • Aggregate or de-anonymize data we provide aggregated for the purpose of identifying individuals.

2.3 Security abuse

  • Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Services without coordinated disclosure (we run a responsible-disclosure program at security@cwleaders.com);
  • Circumvent authentication, authorization, rate-limit, or quota mechanisms;
  • Reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract the source code of the Services or desktop app;
  • Mount denial-of-service attacks, send spam, or abuse free-tier resources at scale.

2.4 Content prohibitions (UGC)

  • Upload, share, or generate content that contains: CSAM; non-consensual intimate imagery; credible threats of violence; instructions to produce weapons of mass harm; doxxing or stalking content; content promoting self-harm to vulnerable populations;
  • Engage in coordinated harassment campaigns or hate speech targeting protected classes;
  • Post commercial messages, spam, chain letters, or pyramid schemes via share links.

2.5 AI-specific prohibitions

  • Use AI agents to impersonate real people in a way intended to deceive;
  • Use AI to generate content claiming false authorship by a real person;
  • Submit prompts that attempt to extract, override, or manipulate model safety guardrails;
  • Use AI agents in any application meeting the "high-risk" classification under the EU AI Act (e.g., critical-infrastructure decision-making, biometric identification of natural persons, law-enforcement profiling).

2.6 MyHire-specific prohibitions

  • Use MyHire to discriminate on the basis of any protected class under EEOC, GDPR, or local law;
  • Conduct deceptive practices (fake job postings, data harvesting, sham interviews);
  • Sell, share, or process applicant data outside the hiring purpose for which it was collected.

3. Resource & fair-use limits

Quantitative limits per tier (see /pricing) include: storage caps, AI credits, share-link counts, recording length, file size. Exceeding limits may result in throttling. We don't enforce limits punitively — we'll always notify before action.

4. Reporting violations

If you observe a violation of this AUP, report it to abuse@cwleaders.com. Include: the URL, the offending content (or hash), the violation category, and your contact information. We respond within 24 hours; CSAM reports within 1 hour during business hours.

5. Enforcement

Violations may result in (in escalating order, depending on severity):

  1. Warning + content removal;
  2. Feature suspension (e.g., share-link disabled);
  3. Account suspension;
  4. Account termination + data destruction (subject to legal hold);
  5. Reporting to law enforcement (mandatory for CSAM, credible violent threats).

Severe violations (CSAM, terrorism content, fraud against us or our users) result in immediate termination without warning. Repeat infringers of intellectual-property rights are terminated in accordance with our DMCA Policy.

6. Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was taken in error, email legal@cwleaders.com with subject "AUP Appeal." We respond within 7 business days. EU/UK users have additional rights under the Digital Services Act.

7. Updates

We may update this AUP. Material changes are notified to active users by email at least 14 days before they take effect.
