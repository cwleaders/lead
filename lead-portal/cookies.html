Cookie Policy.

We use the absolute minimum browser storage needed to keep you signed in and the apps working. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking. Ever.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

What we use

Name Purpose Type Lifetime
lead.token Keeps you signed in to the unified account across CW Leaders properties localStorage (essential) 14 days, refreshed on use
lead.user Caches your display name, plan, and persona for instant page loads localStorage (essential) 14 days, refreshed on use
myhire-application-draft Saves your in-progress job application so you don't lose work if you close the tab sessionStorage (essential) Until tab closes or submission
cw.consent Records your cookie-consent choice so we don't ask again every page load localStorage (essential) 12 months
__Host-* (future) Reserved for migration to HttpOnly cookie auth (improved security; will replace lead.token) Cookie (essential) 14 days

Third-party storage

When you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication writes a small handful of tokens to your browser solely to manage that OAuth handshake. These are essential to the sign-in flow and disappear when you sign out. Firebase's terms apply to that data — see Google's privacy policy.

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies
  • No marketing or remarketing pixels
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No third-party analytics that fingerprint users
  • No social-media tracking widgets

Your control

Because all of our cookies and storage entries are strictly necessary for the Services to function, declining them means you cannot use the Services. We do not require non-essential cookies to use any feature. You can clear our storage at any time from your browser's settings, which will sign you out.

Changes

If we add new storage entries, we'll update this page and (for any non-essential additions) prompt you to consent.
