Accessibility Statement.

Accessibility is a core engineering requirement, not an afterthought. We design and build to WCAG 2.2 Level AA across every CW Leaders property and the desktop app.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

Our commitment

We believe everyone deserves the same access to professional tools, regardless of ability. We design and build the Services to be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.

Standards we target

  • WCAG 2.2 Level AA — across all web properties and the desktop application
  • Section 508 conformance for US federal-procurement contexts
  • EN 301 549 alignment for European customers

What we've built in

  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive element reachable and operable with keyboard alone
  • Screen-reader support — semantic HTML, ARIA labels, live regions for status updates
  • Color contrast — body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1 against backgrounds
  • Focus indicators — visible on all interactive elements
  • Reduced motion — animations honor prefers-reduced-motion media query
  • Resizable text — content works at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling
  • Touch targets — minimum 44×44 CSS pixels on mobile per Apple HIG
  • Form accessibility — every input has an associated label; errors are announced
  • Skip links — keyboard users can skip past navigation to main content
  • Captions and transcripts — generated for video features (Whisper local transcription, free)
  • Safe-area awareness — fixed navigation respects iOS notches and Android system bars

Known limitations and roadmap

We continually audit the Services. Known limitations as of the effective date:

  • Some recently-added desktop-app sidebar tooltips are not yet announced via screen reader (fix scheduled v0.2.0)
  • Mind-Free infinite-canvas drag interactions in Studio require keyboard alternatives — partial today, full keyboard mode in active development
  • The interactive sandbox demo on lead.cwleaders.com requires pointer input; an animated alternative is under design

How to give feedback or get help

If you encounter any accessibility barrier in the Services, please tell us — we treat accessibility reports as P1 issues:

  • Email accessibility@cwleaders.com
  • Describe what you were trying to do and the barrier you hit
  • Include your assistive technology, browser, and operating system if known

We aim to respond within 5 business days and to remediate critical barriers within 30 days.

Alternate formats

If you need any of our policies, contracts, or interface content in an alternate accessible format (large print, plain text, audio, braille-ready, etc.), email accessibility@cwleaders.com and we'll provide it at no cost.

Independent assessment

We engage in periodic independent accessibility audits. Reports and remediation plans are available on request to enterprise customers.
