Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Plain rules. No phone-call requirement. No retention dark patterns. We'd rather have you leave happy than stuck.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you purchase a paid plan (Basic, Powerhouse, Agentic, or Enterprise) and request a refund within 14 days of your initial purchase, we will refund the full amount, no questions asked. Email billing@cwleaders.com with subject "Refund." Funds reach your bank within 5-10 business days depending on your card issuer.

2. After 14 days — pro-rata refund (annual plans only)

If you cancel an annual plan after 14 days but before 30 days, we provide a pro-rata refund for unused months. After 30 days on an annual plan, no refund is issued, but your subscription remains active until the period end.

3. Monthly plans

Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current billing period. You retain access through that date. We do not pro-rate refunds for monthly plans after the 14-day window — but you keep what you paid for.

4. Cancellation process

Cancel anytime, in any of these ways:

  1. Sign in to studio.cwleaders.com/billing and click Cancel subscription (3 clicks total — see Section 6);
  2. Email billing@cwleaders.com with subject "Cancel" — we'll process within 1 business day;
  3. Reply to any billing email with "Cancel" in the body.

We will never require a phone call, video chat, or "retention specialist" interaction to cancel. That's a dark pattern. We don't do it.

5. What happens after cancellation

  • Your subscription remains active until the paid period ends;
  • After expiration, your account drops to the Free tier — your data is preserved, just gated behind paid features;
  • You can re-subscribe at any time and pick up exactly where you left off;
  • If you also delete your account (separate action — see Privacy Policy §8), your data is destroyed within 90 days.

6. Three-click cancel rule

From any signed-in page, the path to a confirmed cancellation is no longer than: Account → Billing → Cancel → Confirm. Maximum 3 active clicks plus a final confirmation. We measure this and treat any drift as a P1 bug.

7. Non-refundable items

  • One-time add-ons that have been delivered (custom AI training credits — N/A in v1, listed for forward coverage);
  • Account fees waived through promotional codes (you can't get cash for a free credit);
  • Stripe processing fees on chargebacks initiated outside this policy.

8. Free trials

Some tiers offer a 7-day free trial. We require a payment method up front (a Stripe norm) but we will email you 48 hours before the trial converts to a paid plan. You can cancel during the trial without any charge.

9. Subscription auto-renewal

Paid plans auto-renew at the end of each billing period at the then-current price for that tier. We'll email you 30 days before any price change. Auto-renewal is required by Stripe for recurring billing; you can cancel auto-renewal anytime via the steps in Section 4.

10. Disputes & chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your bank, please contact us at billing@cwleaders.com. Most issues we can resolve within 24 hours; chargebacks cost us a fee plus damage your future ability to use Stripe-powered services.

11. EU/UK consumer rights

If you are a consumer in the EU/UK, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal under EU Directive 2011/83/EU and equivalent UK law, regardless of this policy. To exercise it, email billing@cwleaders.com within 14 days of the contract start. [LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED for jurisdiction-specific consumer protections.]
