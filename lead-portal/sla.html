Service Level Agreement.

Applies only to paid Powerhouse, Agentic, and Enterprise tiers. We commit to 99.9% monthly uptime on the cloud Services that power those plans. The Free tier is best-effort with no SLA.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

Enterprise 99.95% P1 1h, P2 4h, P3 1d

2. Measurement methodology

"Monthly uptime" is calculated as the percentage of one-minute intervals in a calendar month during which the API endpoint https://api.cwleaders.com/health returned HTTP 200 within 5 seconds. Measurement is from our public synthetic-monitoring system, sampling at 1-minute intervals. Maintenance windows are excluded per Section 3.

3. Exclusions

Downtime caused by the following is excluded:

  • Scheduled maintenance: Announced at least 7 days in advance via the in-app banner and email; capped at 4 hours per month and scheduled outside US-business hours;
  • Force majeure: Acts of God, war, terrorism, government action, internet failures outside our control;
  • Customer-caused downtime: Customer's misuse, customer-side network issues, customer's own integrations;
  • Third-party SaaS dependencies: Stripe, Firebase, AI providers — outages there are credited only if they exceed 4 hours and the affected feature is unusable;
  • Beta features: Anything labeled "Beta" or "Preview" is excluded from SLA.

5. How to claim a credit

Within 30 days of the month in which the SLA was missed, email billing@cwleaders.com with subject "SLA Credit" and include: the affected month, the incident IDs (we publish at /status), and your customer ID. We respond within 5 business days.

6. Sole remedy

Service credits are your sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the SLA. Credits do not expire as long as your subscription remains active. If your subscription terminates, accrued credits are forfeit.
