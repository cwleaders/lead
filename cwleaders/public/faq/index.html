Applicant FAQ

Answers before you apply.

MyHire is built to feel clear and modern. These are the most common questions we hear from applicants before they submit.

Can I apply generally or for a specific opening?

Both are available. You can submit a general talent application through the main apply page or apply through a role-specific opening page such as Strategist and Operationalist 1.

Who should use this site?

Candidates interested in strategic, operational, executive support, chief of staff, logistics, program, or other high-trust functions should use this subdomain. Company clients looking to engage CW Leaders for hiring should use the main CW Leaders site.

What documents do you accept?

MyHire accepts PDF, DOC, and DOCX uploads for resumes and optional cover letters. The current upload limit is 5 MB per file.

Will I receive a confirmation?

Yes. After a successful submission, you will land on a thank-you page and receive a simple confirmation email.

Where is CW Leaders based?

CW Leaders is based in Los Angeles, California and operates globally. MyHire is built to support talent discovery across regions, time zones, and work models.

How do I contact the applicant team?

Use newapp@cwleaders.com if you need to correct or update something after submitting.
