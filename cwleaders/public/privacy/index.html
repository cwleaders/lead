Privacy notice

How applicant information is handled.

MyHire collects only the information needed to review applicants for CW Leaders opportunities and communicate with candidates appropriately.

What we collect

We collect the information you choose to submit through the application form, including contact details, location, professional context, narrative responses, uploaded documents, and consent acknowledgments.

How we use applicant information

We use your submission to evaluate your fit for current or future opportunities led by CW Leaders, contact you regarding relevant roles, and maintain internal records of your application.

Storage and access

Applicant materials are transmitted through a secure form endpoint and stored in private internal systems or private cloud storage. Access is limited to authorized CW Leaders personnel and trusted service providers involved in application handling.

Communications

After you submit, MyHire may send a confirmation email. CW Leaders may contact you about your application or related opportunities. We do not promise response timelines or interview progression through this site.

Retention

We retain applicant information for as long as reasonably necessary to review talent, support related hiring activity, meet operational requirements, and satisfy legal obligations where applicable.

Contact

Questions about this notice or requests to update applicant information can be sent to newapp@cwleaders.com.

MyHire by CW Leaders is based in Los Angeles, California and operates globally for applicant discovery.
